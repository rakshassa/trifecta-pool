Terms & Conditions for Trifecta BCA Pool League SMS Program

Effective Date: September 4, 2026

These Terms & Conditions govern participation in the Trifecta BCA Pool League SMS notification program.

1. SMS Program
The Trifecta BCA Pool League SMS program provides league participants with recurring text-message notifications related to their BCA pool league participation.
Messages may include:
Upcoming league match dates
Opposing team information
Assigned pool tables
Other league-related information

2. How to Opt In
To request enrollment, a team captain sends the keyword JOIN by text message to the Trifecta BCA Pool League SMS number.
After receiving JOIN, Trifecta BCA Pool League sends a response explaining the SMS program and providing the required disclosures, including the expected message frequency, the statement that message and data rates may apply, and instructions for opting out.
The recipient must then reply YES to confirm enrollment. Sending JOIN alone does not enroll the recipient in recurring SMS messages.
By replying YES, the recipient affirmatively consents to receive recurring SMS messages from Trifecta BCA Pool League at the mobile number used to send YES.

3. Message Frequency
You can expect approximately one SMS message per week during the pool league season. Message frequency may vary if additional league-related information needs to be communicated.

4. Consent
Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.
You may choose not to enroll in the SMS program or may opt out at any time.

5. Message and Data Rates
Message and data rates may apply. Charges for SMS messages are determined by your mobile carrier and are your responsibility.

6. How to Opt Out
You may stop receiving SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message from Trifecta BCA Pool League.
After submitting an opt-out request, you will no longer receive recurring league-related SMS messages unless you subsequently provide consent to receive them again.

7. Help
For assistance with the SMS program, reply HELP to a Trifecta BCA Pool League SMS message or contact Trifecta BCA Pool League directly.

8. Supported Carriers
SMS service may not be available on all mobile carriers or in all areas. Trifecta BCA Pool League does not guarantee delivery of every message, as delivery may be affected by factors outside our control, including mobile carrier networks and coverage.

9. Privacy
Trifecta BCA Pool League respects your privacy.
Mobile phone numbers collected for this SMS program will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their own marketing or promotional purposes.
Mobile phone numbers may be shared with service providers as necessary to operate the SMS program, such as the SMS messaging provider.
Information about the collection and use of personal information is provided in the Trifecta BCA Pool League Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to These Terms
Trifecta BCA Pool League may update these Terms & Conditions from time to time. Any changes will be posted with an updated effective date.
