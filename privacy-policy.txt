Privacy Policy for Trifecta BCA Pool League

Effective Date: September 3, 2026

Trifecta BCA Pool League (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use mobile phone numbers in connection with our SMS notification service.

Information We Collect
We collect mobile phone numbers from league participants who choose to receive SMS notifications regarding their upcoming pool league matches and table assignments.
We may also collect the team name associated with a mobile phone number for the purpose of delivering the appropriate match and table-assignment information.

How We Use Mobile Phone Numbers
Mobile phone numbers are used solely to send SMS notifications related to pool league matches, including information such as:
Upcoming match dates
Opposing team
Assigned pool tables
Other necessary information related to the participant's scheduled league match

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile phone numbers will not be shared with third parties except as necessary to provide the requested SMS service, such as our SMS service provider.
Message Frequency

Participants should expect to receive approximately one SMS message per week, relating to their upcoming league match.
Message frequency may vary if additional notifications are necessary to communicate important league-related information.

Message and Data Rates
Message and data rates may apply. The cost of SMS messages and data usage depends on your mobile carrier and cellular plan. We are not responsible for carrier charges.

Opting Out
You may stop receiving SMS messages at any time by texting STOP or contacting us and requesting removal of your mobile phone number from our SMS notification service.
If you have questions about SMS messages or wish to update or remove your phone number, please contact us using the contact information provided below.

Data Security
We take reasonable measures to protect the mobile phone numbers and other information we maintain from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted with an updated effective date.
